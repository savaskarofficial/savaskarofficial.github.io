Privacy Policy

Covert — HTTP Debugger & API Client
Effective Date: March 25, 2026  |  Last Updated: November 23, 2026

1. Introduction

Undercover IT Firm (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates the Covert mobile application (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App.

Delete your Covert account or delete Cloud Sync data.

2. Information We Collect

When you create or access an account, we collect the account information you provide, including your email address, display name and sign-in details. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google profile name, email address and profile picture URL.

When you sign in, we collect device information such as the model, operating system and App version, along with a Covert installation identifier for managing your account, license and device limits.

When you make a Google Play purchase, Google Play processes the payment. Covert sends the product ID and purchase token to our service to verify the purchase and manage your access.

When you start capture, Covert reads network traffic on your device so you can inspect it. Captured records can include addresses, the source app, headers, cookies, and available request and response contents. This information can include personal or sensitive information sent by the apps you capture. Covert normally keeps captured records on your device. Cloud Sync and the sharing features described below can send copies outside the device.

When you open the per-app filter, Covert reads the installed-app list to let you choose which apps to include or exclude. The filter uses that list on the device. A captured record may identify its source app, and that information can travel with the record if you sync or share it.

For eligible signed-in accounts, Cloud Sync can upload Favorites, including bookmarked requests and responses, and workspace data such as Collections, Environments, settings, rules and scripts. Automatic sync is on by default for eligible accounts. You can turn it off in Cloud Sync settings or choose to sync manually.

When you scan a collaboration QR code, Covert uses Google ML Kit to read the barcode on the device. Google documents that its SDK may send installation identifiers, app and device information, feature events and performance diagnostics over HTTPS for usage analytics and diagnostics.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the App and your account.
  • To show the traffic you capture and provide the Cloud Sync, collaboration, reporting and export features you use.
  • To manage your subscription, verify purchases and enforce device limits.
  • To send transactional emails about password resets, account registration and subscription status.
  • To provide customer support.

4. VPN Service

The App uses Android’s VpnService API to create a local connection on your device for network debugging. Covert does not operate a hosted VPN gateway. Covert explains the capture before it starts and asks for your consent. You can stop capture at any time. Without a per-app filter, capture can include traffic from apps other than Covert. In the per-app filter you can choose which apps to include or exclude.

Live VPN capture happens on the device. Cloud Sync can copy bookmarked traffic to your Covert account, and the App can send captured data when you use a sharing feature.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or trade your personal information. Google Sign-In provides authentication, Google Play Billing processes Play purchases, and Google ML Kit provides QR code scanning and may send SDK diagnostics and usage data to Google as described above.

When you start a collaboration session, connected peers can receive captured traffic while the session is active. When you turn on the Report Server, someone on a network that can reach your device can view traffic reports if they have the access link. When you export or share a capture, the destination you choose receives the data included in the export or share.

6. Data Storage and Security

Covert stores ordinary captures in the App’s private storage on your device. Account data and Cloud Sync copies are stored on our servers, and the App connects to these services over HTTPS. Collaboration uses an encrypted connection. The optional LAN Report Server uses a token-protected HTTP link. That HTTP connection does not encrypt the report traffic.

You can clear captured traffic from within the App. Clearing the local capture does not remove copies that were synced, exported or shared. See Delete Cloud Sync data for copies stored in your Covert account.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active.

Delete your Covert account

In Covert, open your profile, select Delete Account and confirm. You can also request account deletion without the App by emailing admin@undercoveritfirm.com with the email address on your Covert account. Upon deletion, we permanently remove the personal data associated with your account from our servers within 30 days.

Delete Cloud Sync data and keep your account

In Covert, open Cloud Sync. Use the remove button next to a data type to delete its server copy, or choose Delete everything from the server under Server copy and confirm. This keeps the local copies on your devices. If you cannot access the App, email admin@undercoveritfirm.com with the email address on your Covert account and what you want removed.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.
